KhetiQ Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · KhetiQ by RPJP Organics Pvt. Ltd.

KhetiQ · Terms of Service

KhetiQ helps farmers record crops, stock, daily sales and expenses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile photo from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile: full name, phone number, city, state and role (for example farmer), which you enter.
  • Farm records you enter: crop, stock and other entries (name, farm name, start date, sale unit, active status), daily records (date, activity, mandi name, quantity, revenue), expenses (name and amount) and stock items (name, type, purchase date, quantity, unit, status, remark, expense), including a history of stock quantity and status changes.
  • On your phone only: your dark mode choice, and changes made without internet until they upload.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your dashboard, totals and reports, and to create the PDF, Excel or CSV exports you request.
  • To suggest names while you type. You see your own earlier activity, mandi and expense names. You may also see names that at least two different KhetiQ users have entered; these are shown without any amounts, dates, crops or who entered them.

Who can see your data

Only you. Each account can read and change only its own records; this is enforced by the database, not just the app. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Exported files are shared only where you choose to send them.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google sign-in is provided by Google.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleted entries stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, then are removed. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account menu → Account settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile and all your entries, records, expenses and stock items. You can also delete individual entries, records and stock items in the app.

Children

KhetiQ is meant for farmers and farm businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
