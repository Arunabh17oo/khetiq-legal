Delete your KhetiQ account

KhetiQ by RPJP Organics Pvt. Ltd.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open KhetiQ and tap your photo at the top of Home.
  2. Tap Account settings, scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Type Delete and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email rpjporganics@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject Delete my KhetiQ account. We will delete your account and confirm by email, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, phone number, city, state, role).
  • All your crop, stock and other entries, daily records, expenses, stock items and stock history, including anything in Recently deleted.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Names you typed that other users had also used (for example a mandi name) may still appear as suggestions, because they belong to those users’ records too; they are never linked to you.
